# 🧠 vcp - Annotate Live Sites, Supercharge AI Agents

---

## 🚀 What Is vcp?

Think of vcp as a **digital highlighter for websites** — but smarter. It lets you click, point, and annotate anything on a live webpage. Those notes stay there when you refresh, when you switch pages, and when you come back later. And the best part? It connects straight into **Antigravity AI agents**, so you can show an AI exactly what you see and what you want changed — no coding required.

Whether you're a designer reviewing a website, a manager giving feedback, or someone testing an app, vcp helps you mark up pages visually and hand that context over to AI tools. No confusing code. Just click, type, and go.

---

## ✨ Key Features

Here's what makes vcp surprisingly powerful — even if you've never written a line of code:

- **🖱️ Point & Click Annotation**  
  Click any element on a webpage and add a note. A small, friendly marker appears right there. Perfect for saying "move this button up" or "this text is hard to read."

- **💾 Persistent Notes Across Pages**  
  Your annotations don't vanish. vcp remembers them as you navigate between pages on the same site. Come back tomorrow — they're still there.

- **🤖 Antigravity AI Bridge**  
  With one click, vcp packages every annotation and sends it to Antigravity AI agents. The AI sees exactly what you see — real page elements, real locations, real context — and can work on your comments directly.

- **🔒 Runs Inside Your Browser**  
  Built as a Chrome extension with modern Manifest V3 standards. It works in the background without slowing down your computer or hogging memory.

- **🛡️ Privacy-Friendly Shadow DOM Technology**  
  Your notes live in a "shadow" layer — they don't interfere with the website's actual code or layout. Nothing breaks. Nothing changes visibility for other visitors.

- **🎯 Built for Real-World Workflows**  
  Pair programming, UI reviews, QA testing, accessibility checklists — if you look at websites, you have a use case.

## 🚀 Getting Started (First 5 Minutes)

Let's do a quick practice run so you understand the magic:

1. **Open any website** — say your company's homepage or a news site.  
2. **Click the vcp icon** in your toolbar. The icon turns active, meaning annotation mode is on.  
3. **Click any text or button** on the page. A small box appears where you can type a note.  
4. **Write something** like "Change this heading to blue." Click **Save**.  
5. **Notice the marker** — a small dot or icon stays on that element.  
6. **Click the vcp icon again** to turn off annotation mode. The page looks normal, but your notes are saved.

Now, refresh the page. Your marker is still there. That's persistence in action.

## 🧩 Using vcp with Antigravity AI Agents

Here's how vcp becomes part of your daily workflow:

1. **Annotate a webpage** using the steps above. Add as many notes as you want — you can annotate 1 element or 50.  
2. **Open Antigravity AI Agent** (if you don't have it, the extension will guide you).  
3. **Click the "Send to AI" button** inside the vcp panel.  
4. Your annotations are packaged together as **visual context** and delivered to the AI. The AI can now reference exact locations, colors, text, and layout positions — because it "sees" what you see.  
5. **Ask the AI** to fix, improve, or generate code based on your notes. The AI understands exactly what you meant.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting (Common Fixes)

| Problem | Solution |
|--------|----------|
| **I don't see the vcp icon after installation** | Go to `chrome://extensions`, find vcp, and enable it. Then click the puzzle piece icon in the toolbar to pin vcp. |
| **My annotations disappeared** | Make sure you're on the same domain (e.g., `example.com` vs `www.example.com`). Switch off annotation mode, refresh the page, and switch it back on. |
| **The "Send to AI" button is grayed out** | Check your internet connection. Also, make sure Antigravity AI is installed or logged in — vcp will show a guide if something's missing. |
| **The extension slows down Chrome** | vcp only activates when you click its icon. If performance bothers you, simply turn off annotation mode when you're done. That's it. |
| **Windows says "Unknown Publisher"** | This is normal. Click "More Info" → "Run Anyway." vcp is open-source, and Windows just hasn't seen it before. |

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is vcp free?**  
A: Yes. It's an open-source project — free forever, no subscription.

**Q: Does vcp change the website?**  
A: No. All annotations live in a shadow layer that's invisible to the site itself. Other visitors see nothing. The website's code and behavior stay exactly the same.

**Q: Can I delete a note?**  
A: Absolutely. In annotation mode, click any marker and choose "Delete note." To remove all notes on a page, open the vcp panel and click "Clear Page."

**Q: Does vcp work in other browsers?**  
A: vcp works on any Chromium browser — Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi. Safari and Firefox are not supported yet.

**Q: Where are my notes stored?**  
A: Notes live in your local browser storage. They are not sent to the cloud unless you explicitly click "Send to AI."
